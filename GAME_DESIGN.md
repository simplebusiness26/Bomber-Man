# Blast Grid — Game Design Blueprint

## Core promise
A round should be understandable in seconds: move through a compact grid, create safe routes with timed bombs, engineer chain reactions, out-think enemy movement and reach the exit. Depth comes from positioning and bomb technology rather than complicated controls.

## Campaign
Five sectors contain six stages each. Stages 1–5 escalate enemy mix, block density and upgrade access. Stage 6 is a Warden fight. Clearing a Warden permanently introduces a new piece of bomb technology into the run's possible toolkit.

1. **Neon Yard** — fundamentals, Drifters and Hunters.
2. **Frost Foundry** — phasing Orbs and more constrained routes.
3. **Moss Circuit** — line-firing Turrets and denser soft blocks.
4. **Ember Vault** — fast Sparks and mixed enemy pressure.
5. **Void Array** — full-system combinations and final Warden.

## Bomb technologies
- **Fuse** — standard timed cross blast.
- **Pulse** — waits until ACTION remotely triggers it.
- **Pierce** — blast can continue through one destructible block in each direction.
- **Frost** — shorter fuse, blue blast, briefly freezes surviving targets.
- **Mine** — armed proximity bomb unlocked from a Warden.

## Upgrades
- Blast range.
- Simultaneous bomb capacity.
- Movement speed.
- Kick: walking into a bomb slides it along the grid.
- Pulse, Pierce, Frost and Mine technology unlocks.
- Shield charges and extra lives.

## Enemy roles
- **Drifter** — readable roaming pressure.
- **Hunter** — tends toward the player.
- **Orb** — can phase through destructible blocks.
- **Turret** — fires down a clear row/column.
- **Spark** — fast unpredictable mover.
- **Warden** — multi-hit sector boss that pursues the player and places hostile bombs.

## Mobile design rules
- Landscape only so the complete arena and both thumb zones remain visible.
- No precision swipes required.
- D-pad movement is grid-snapped and repeatable while held.
- Primary DROP button is oversized and isolated from secondary controls.
- Game is fully playable offline and does not require an account.

## Future expansion hooks
The engine is intentionally data-driven enough to add arena multiplayer, handcrafted challenge stages, daily seeded runs, cosmetics and additional enemy/bomb behaviours without replacing the core loop.
